Privacy Policy & Compliance

Last updated: November 2026

Welcome to FinCalC. We respect your privacy and are fully committed to protecting your personal information while you use our high-utility calculation suite.

1. Information We Collect

Our calculation tools operate entirely client-side within your web browser. Numeric values and inputs you provide are processed dynamically and are never stored or saved on any remote server.
